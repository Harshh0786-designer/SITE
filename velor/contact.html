Car Story buys, restores and sells a small number of cars a year. We would rather you stood next to one than read about it, so everything below is arranged around getting you in front of the metal.

Who we are

We started in 2016 with one workshop bay in Lower Parel and a stubborn view that the used-car trade had the priorities backwards: too many cars, too little known about any of them. So we hold fewer. Every car on our floor has been bought outright by us, stripped back far enough to know what we own, and put back together by the people who inspected it.

We do not broker cars we have not physically had in our own workshop. If a car is on the floor, it is ours, it is here, and you can drive it.

How we work

A car arrives, and before it is priced it goes through a full mechanical and cosmetic inspection — around forty hours on an older car. Whatever that turns up is fixed with factory-supplied parts, and the invoices stay with the car. Nothing is written off as patina and nothing is quietly deferred to the next owner.

Every car is sold with its full inspection file and parts history. You get the same document we used to decide what the car was worth.

What it costs, and when

Prices are quoted in rupees and are landed figures: import duty, homologation and registration are already accounted for, so the number on the card is the number you pay. We do not add a delivery, documentation or handling fee at the end.

If you are selling, we buy outright or broker on a fixed fee agreed before we inspect. Outright purchases settle within three working days.
